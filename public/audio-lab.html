Privacy and audio safety notice

This lab runs entirely in your browser using the Web Audio API. No audio is uploaded, stored, or sent to Gemini or any other external service. Microphone input, when enabled, is analyzed locally only and is never routed to your speakers. Signal generator output can be loud: start at a low level and use hearing protection when testing at high levels.
